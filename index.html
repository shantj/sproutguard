FAQ

Is this a parental control app?
No — and that's the whole point. SproutGuard is designed for you to block yourself. There's no monitoring dashboard, no reports, no second party.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no backend. Nothing about your usage leaves the device.
How does the blocking work?
It's built on Apple's own Screen Time framework, so app and website blocking happen at the system level, entirely on-device.
Is it free?
Yes. The core — blocking, focus sessions, streaks — is free. Premium — unlimited rules and weekly insights — is an optional auto-renewing subscription. The free tier is not a trial and does not expire.
What do I need to run it?
An iPhone on iOS 18 or later. It's iPhone-only right now — there's no iPad app yet. Worth checking before you tap Get.
What if I just turn it off?
You can. It's your phone and your call. The streak is the point: the friction plus the number you don't want to reset is what actually changes behavior.
