A few things to know.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start using SproutGuard without signing up or logging in.

Why does it ask for Screen Time access?

Apple’s permission allows SproutGuard to block the apps you select and provide usage insights. You remain in control of these permissions in your device settings.

What devices are supported?

SproutGuard is designed for iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Screen Time permission is required for blocking and usage insights.
