FAQ

Is this a parental control app?
No — and that's the whole point. SproutGuard is designed for you to block yourself. There's no monitoring dashboard, no reports, no second party.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no backend. Nothing about your usage leaves the device.
How does the blocking work?
It's built on Apple's own Screen Time framework, so blocking happens at the system level, entirely on-device.
Is it free?
Yes. The core — blocking, focus sessions, streaks — is free. There's an optional Pro upgrade for power features.
What if I just turn it off?
You can. It's your phone and your call. The streak is the point: the friction plus the number you don't want to reset is what actually changes behavior.
